# Attribution

## 3D models

All entity, tile and grid models in `public/models/` are derived from the official Factorio
3D-print models published by **Wube Software** on Printables:

https://www.printables.com/@WubeSoftware/models

License: **Creative Commons — Attribution — Noncommercial (CC BY-NC)**.
The GLB files here are lossy, simplified conversions of the original STLs made for on-screen
rendering only; download the original STLs from Printables for printing.
